All 6 weights (a1,a2,b1,b2,c1,c2) are independently adjustable; the model has 2 free parameters up to overall scale, so Δ alone doesn't capture everything once you leave the symmetric case. Δ>1: ferroelectric. −1≤Δ<1: disordered. Δ<−1: antiferroelectric. Exact sample uses Coupling From The Past for a mathematically exact draw (n≤250). This is verified empirically (thousands of monotone-coupled sweeps, zero ordering violations) at a1=a2=b1=b2=1, not derived from a published theorem -- monotonicity was directly tested and found to break under general weights, so exact sampling is restricted to that regime.
